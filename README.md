# IponTa – Tara, Ipon Ta!

> **IponTa** is a Filipino-focused personal finance and budget tracking platform designed to help users take control of their money, monitor spending, achieve savings goals, and build better financial habits.

---

## 🌟 Brand Identity

- **App Name:** IponTa
- **Tagline:** *Tara, Ipon Ta!*
- **Brand Personality:** Friendly, approachable, modern, and Filipino-centric personal finance companion.
- **Tone:** Encouraging, motivating, and easy to understand for everyday Filipinos managing their money.

---

## 🚀 Key Features

1. **Flexible Budget Cycles & Cutoffs**
   - Semi-monthly (15th & 30th / Katapusan paydays)
   - Monthly and bi-weekly tracking
   - Responsive cycle switcher with custom cutoff support

2. **Payday Budgeter**
   - Income distribution planning
   - Emergency fund and savings allocation
   - Zero-based budgeting support

3. **Installments & PayLater Monitor (BNPL)**
   - Track installments for SpayLater, LazPayLater, Atome, credit card monthly amortizations
   - Accurate interest calculation and remaining term tracking

4. **Accounts & E-Wallets Hub**
   - GCash, Maya, SeaBank, Gotyme, BPI, BDO, RCBC, cash vaults
   - Real-time balance calculations across accounts

5. **Household & Shared Budgets**
   - Split bills, track shared expenses, and allocate contributions among family or roommates

6. **Privacy & Offline-First Storage**
   - Seamless local-first storage with optional Supabase cloud synchronization
   - Export and backup datasets anytime in JSON format
   - Printable / downloadable PDF financial summary reports

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- **Frontend:** React 18, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Lucide Icons
- **Bundler & Build Tool:** Vite
- **Testing:** Vitest
- **Data Persistence:** Offline-first local storage with optional Supabase integration
